## What the profile encodes

The eight dimensions follow the source scorecard exactly: Human Connection &
Trust (15), Discovery & Deep Understanding (20), Qualification (15), Problem,
Impact & Desire Clarity (10), Solution Relevance & Presentation (10), Certainty
& Objection Intelligence (15), Closing & Decision Management (5), and
Communication & Tonality (5). Each dimension carries exact Doc/section
citations in the JSON.

The evaluation instruction requires observable evidence spans, preserves
`unknown`, `insufficient_evidence`, `not_applicable` and `conflicted`, and judges
the purpose of a behavior rather than a literal question, fixed order or
question count. This follows Doc-1 §17 and §54–§56, Doc-3 §1 and §43–§49, and
Doc-5 §2 and §41–§43.

The context rules explicitly cover price diagnosis (Doc-3 §28–§31; Doc-4
§8–§14; Doc-5 §22–§25), genuine decision-makers (Doc-3 §32; Doc-4 §17–§20;
Doc-5 §27), legitimate future dates (Doc-1 §47–§49; Doc-3 §36; Doc-5 §32 and
§40), when to push or back off (Doc-1 §37, §40 and §48–§49; Doc-4 §38–§40,
§50 and §56), and silence or interruptions (Doc-2 §9–§12 and §31; Doc-5
§33–§37).

## Safety and interpretation boundaries

- A speaker label is uncertain unless separately adjudicated. The profile does
  not identify a person from a voice and does not infer hidden motives.
- Tonality guidance is limited to observed pacing, pauses, overlap and
  composure when the permitted evidence supports them. It does not infer a
  fixed trait, personality or stable voice quality from one call.
- A closed deal is not proof of quality and a lost deal is not proof of failure
  (Doc-5 §39–§40). A legitimate future decision can be a successful call.
- Ethics flags deception, fabricated claims or proof, false guarantees,
  manipulation, coercion, manufactured fear or urgency, concealment and knowingly
  unsuitable selling (Doc-5 §38; Doc-4 §61–§62).
- Feedback is behavioral and actionable. The default report gives no more than
  three primary improvements, each with behavior, evidence, consequence,
  correction and practice instruction (Doc-5 §48–§52).
