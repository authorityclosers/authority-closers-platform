The HMAC-SHA256 envelope has exactly `record` and `hmac_sha256`; the MAC covers sorted compact UTF-8 JSON of the complete record. Candidate and base are compared for exact equality, not ancestry equivalence or migration-tree equivalence. The producer checks base ancestry and derives the exact base head from its snapshot. Manifest/dump and source pins cannot be taken from candidate text. Only successful trusted rehearsal creates a receipt.

Receipts are appended with candidate/base plus UTC microsecond timestamp and nonce, using O_EXCL, restrictive mode and fsync. Prior audit evidence is never overwritten. Verification reads only the latest record in the fixed protected candidate/base namespace; it rejects missing, malformed, wrong-MAC, failed, mismatched candidate/base, wrong bundle/image, invalid identity/digests, future-dated or stale evidence. A receipt expires one hour after completion, and evidence expires 24 hours after export. The engine derives the exact deployment candidate and current staging base independently and checks the base again after verification. The producer must use these same commits, including the actual merge SHA. Changing trusted code/image/key invalidates old evidence. No receipt is a production deployment claim or permission.
