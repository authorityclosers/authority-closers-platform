## Exact pilot policy

Focus is optional, distinct from earned credits/XP, and activated explicitly
before the first answer: once per attempt, one active run per tenant/person.
It starts with three charges, capped at three. A deliberate confirmed end costs
one only after the first feedback acknowledgment; before that it costs zero.
Pause, page close, disconnection and wrong answers cost zero. Saved responses
remain resumable in standard mode. Completing an active Focus run restores one,
capped at three; the next saved-timezone practice day resets to three under the
existing timezone policy without rebucketing history. At zero, only new Focus
runs are unavailable: standard practice and earned rewards remain available.
No purchased refill, earned-credit deduction or XP deduction is introduced.

## Research boundary

The design is an informed pilot, not a scientifically optimized three/one
formula. Duolingo reports that mistake-based hearts discouraged beginners and
that its energy tests improved lesson completion—not demonstrated proficiency.
[First-party case study](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/).
Loss framing improved walking-goal achievement during one randomized incentive
trial, but follow-up steps were not different from control; that is not evidence
for learning-exit penalties. [Patel et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26881417/).
A learning-gamification meta-analysis found positive average outcomes with
heterogeneous interventions; higher-rigor motivational/behavioral estimates were
not significant. [Sailer and Homner](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-019-09498-w).
Inference: preserve choice, mistake safety and earned rewards; measure learning
and frustration separately from completion and engagement.
