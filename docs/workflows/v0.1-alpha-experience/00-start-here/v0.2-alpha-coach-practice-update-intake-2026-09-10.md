# v0.2 Alpha: Coach, adaptive practice and release updates

Authority: direct user requests in the active release task on 10 September 2026.
This extends the existing release sequence; it is not a release announcement or
permission to bypass the controlled security, content and deployment gates.

## Ship order

1. Usable free course: real authenticated video playback and Coach preview,
   canonical authoring access, verified upload/processing, and truthful recovery.
2. Practical Coach authoring: course outline, reusable lesson templates, readings,
   interactive checks, artwork, preview and publication in one coherent workflow.
3. Mixed daily practice and authored natural-language variants, with useful
   learner feedback and academy-scoped diagnostics.
4. Draft commercial offers and provider adapters; activate prices, checkout and
   subscriptions only after commercial/provider terms are approved.

Notifications are a parallel bounded slice: package-owned release notes and
server-saved, account/academy-scoped read receipts. They must not announce video,
payments or other unfinished features as live. No marketing email/push activated.

## Required practice outcomes

- Easily selectable English, Hindi, Marathi, Hinglish and Marathi-English
  (Marlish). Natural spoken, editorially reviewed language, not literal machine
  translation; separate global and Indian/Maharashtrian scenario registers.
- Longer daily sessions mixing activity families. Difficulty and graduated hints
  can scaffold/prefill supported pieces after repeated misses. Accessibility and
  free retry must remain usable.
- Review scheduling informed by canonical response history, elapsed visible-active
  time and previous practice. Research established spaced repetition before
  selecting a bounded algorithm; do not market an unvalidated scheduler as Anki.
- Time per question does not explain *why* a learner struggled. Avoid sensitive
  profiling, full keystroke capture, and official autonomous AI scoring.
- Conversation activities preserve scrollable chat history and conversational
  choices, without hiding preceding dialogue behind a dropdown. This is an
  in-app exercise, not a WhatsApp integration.
- Keep the approved earned-only reward rules; changes to difficulty cannot silently
  alter credits, XP, paid access, certificates or formal assessment semantics.

## Commercial boundary

Subscriptions and one-off courses/quizzes are requested, but prices, tax,
renewal/cancellation and commercial terms are not yet approved. Preserve the
provider adapter boundary for Razorpay/Cashfree/PayU; provider payment state must
not directly grant access. Public offer activation and real charges remain off.

## Research observations, not adopted business rules

Checked official documentation on 10 September 2026:

- [Graphy video authoring](https://us.help.graphy.com/support/solutions/articles/1060000125258-how-to-add-a-video-to-your-course-)
  documents upload/processing, captions, editable thumbnails and video files up
  to 5 GB. Our acceptance target remains measured 1–2 GiB resumable uploads.
- [Graphy course builder](https://us.help.graphy.com/support/solutions/articles/1060000131284-how-to-create-course-and-upload-content-on-graphy-)
  groups chapters, upload/create/reuse content and publication. The useful design
  inference is one outline-led workflow, not copying its pricing or integrations.
- [Thinkific course builder](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/37783573725463-How-to-Add-Content-and-Configure-Your-Course-in-the-New-Course-Builder)
  documents composing lessons from content blocks and retaining uploaded video in
  a library. Reusable safe blocks are a better first scope than user-written code.
