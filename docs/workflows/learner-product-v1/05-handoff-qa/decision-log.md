# Decision log

## DEC-LV1-001 — Full learner product surface inventory

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: selected for contract reconciliation

Dashboard, My Learning, Discover, Progress, Notifications, Profile, and
Settings are distinct surfaces. Desktop and compact navigation have different
compositions while sharing route semantics.

## DEC-LV1-002 — Light default

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: founder direction; predecessor theme contract requires supersession

Light is the initial/default product presentation. Dark/System may remain as
later user preferences. Theme never changes permissions, routes, content,
progress, or access.

## DEC-LV1-003 — VPS-first provider-neutral media

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: proposed; blocked by media/governance/operational gates

The product depends on a stable playback-session contract and
`MediaDeliveryPort`. The initial adapter may use the VPS; a later CDN adapter
must be replaceable without changing activity routes or domain semantics.

## DEC-LV1-004 — Deterministic low-typing practice

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: proposed; interaction schema contract required

Module 1 post-video activities use authored choice, ordering, matching,
branching, evidence-chip, and confidence interactions. Optional notes are
secondary. No autonomous scoring, mastery, rewards, ranks, or streak claims.

## DEC-LV1-005 — Visual system selection

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: selected

Exactly three paired desktop/mobile directions were generated. Direction A is
the global shell/Dashboard language, Direction B is the learning/practice
subsystem, and Direction C is the media/Discover treatment after API readiness.
The visual grammar is not blended: A remains authoritative and B/C contribute
bounded patterns. Exact rationale and gates are in
`03-visual-exploration/selection.md`.

## DEC-LV1-006 — Dipak asset provenance gate

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: blocked — no new generation

The exact controlled-source register and required source order were checked
before starting the asset pipeline. The connected Google Drive connector was
callable, but no standalone Dipak portrait/headshot with an exact approved
Drive ID/URL was present in the repository manifests or returned by image
search. The raw founder direction PDF and visual prompt pack are context and
generation guidance; neither is a photo rights/consent record. The public
homepage at `https://dipakvishwakarma.com/` confirms founder context and exposes
portrait paths, but it is public evidence only and does not establish consent,
derivative-use permission, license, retention, or a controlled source record.

No ImageGen job was issued. The existing
`apps/learner-web/public/media/dipak-learning-hero-v1.png` is preserved and
quarantined as public-evidence-derived historical staging artwork. Requested
desktop/mobile hero/poster compositions, course thumbnail pair, and square
profile avatar are recorded as `blocked_provenance` in the asset manifest. They
may be generated only after an exact Drive source ID/URL, written subject/owner
consent, rights/license scope, retention/takedown metadata, and crop/identity
review are recorded. The dedicated evidence and unblock list are in
`dipak-asset-provenance-gap.md`.
