# Learner screen-family experience brief

## Problem

Learners need a calm, directional surface that makes the next safe action
obvious across discovery, structured learning, practice, evidence, progress,
and recovery. A polished dashboard without truthful state and recovery would
increase confusion and support burden.

## Audience

Primary: mobile-heavy, time-poor, self-directed learners with mixed experience
levels. Secondary: learners using keyboard, screen reader, zoom, poor networks,
or an installed PWA. Recovery audience: scoped support/domain operators who
diagnose routine identity, access, playback, draft, progress, notification, or
certificate cases without raw database editing.

## Desired first value

After safe authentication and optional bounded onboarding, a learner should be
able to understand where they are, choose the next eligible action, and reach
the first configured activity quickly. The controlled foundation uses a roughly
15-minute First Win target as a measurable hypothesis, not a guarantee or
mastery claim.

## Product surfaces

Dashboard/home, My Learning, Discover/catalog, public program preview, enrolled
program detail, module/path map, activity/evidence loop, activity-owned media,
Progress, descriptive Insights, Today/Week/Month plan horizons, Notifications,
Profile/avatar crop, Settings/theme/session, Certificate, and cross-cutting
auth/onboarding/expiry/offline/retry surfaces.

## Experience principles

1. Truthful next action beats decorative density.
2. Canonical facts, presentation state, and recovery ownership remain distinct.
3. Every mutation preserves safe input and reports its durable result.
4. Missing, stale, partial, locked, denied, processing, and terminal states are
   understandable and actionable.
5. Low-typing activity controls support practice without inventing a scoring
   taxonomy or claiming mastery.
6. Light-default presentation and local theme variants must not alter authority.
7. Desktop and mobile share semantics but use purpose-built composition.

## Success evidence required later

State-matrix contract review, unit/API/authorization tests, injected failure and
resume tests, keyboard/screen-reader/zoom/touch checks, exact-current browser/
PWA evidence, approved media/caption/avatar/certificate contracts, and exact-SHA
release proof. This brief alone is not runtime approval.
