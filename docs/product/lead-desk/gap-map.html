Where the CRM goes beyond NeoDove

Fair load, with a reason

NeoDove splits evenly or by column rules. The CRM also counts each seller's open leads and cap, and every lead says why it went to that person.

Every call becomes a Report

A desk call goes through Sales Xray's analysis, and its Report sits on the lead. NeoDove stores the recording and talk time.

Nothing from the source is lost

Each lead keeps the exact fields its source sent, so a new Meta question or sheet column needs no schema change.
