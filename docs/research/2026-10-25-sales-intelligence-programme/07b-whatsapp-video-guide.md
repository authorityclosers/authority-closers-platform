# 07b — Running a test on WhatsApp video (one page, for Dipak)

*Part of the usability test kit (chapter 7). About 35 minutes per tester. You need: your
laptop with WhatsApp Desktop (or a second phone), the kit's tasks (`07`), the forms (`07a`)
and the score sheet (`07c`).*

**The day before**
1. Pick 5–8 testers: at least 3 who prefer Hindi or Marathi, different phone makers, Android
   only. Agree a 35-minute slot.
2. Send this message (change the language to theirs):
   > EN: "Thank you for testing Sales X-Ray! Tomorrow at <time> I'll video-call you on WhatsApp
   > for about 30 minutes. You'll try a few tasks in the app while sharing your phone screen.
   > We test the app, not you. If you agree, I'll record the shared screen and our voices only
   > for the team, kept 90 days. You can say no or stop any time."
   > HI: "Sales X-Ray टेस्ट करने के लिए धन्यवाद! कल <समय> पर मैं WhatsApp पर करीब 30 मिनट का
   > वीडियो कॉल करूँगा/करूँगी। आप फ़ोन की स्क्रीन शेयर करके ऐप में कुछ काम करके देखेंगे। हम ऐप को
   > टेस्ट करते हैं, आपको नहीं। आपकी अनुमति हो तो सिर्फ़ टीम के लिए शेयर की हुई स्क्रीन और हमारी
   > आवाज़ रिकॉर्ड होगी, 90 दिन तक रखी जाएगी। आप कभी भी मना कर सकते हैं या रोक सकते हैं।"
   > MR: "Sales X-Ray टेस्ट केल्याबद्दल धन्यवाद! उद्या <वेळ> ला मी WhatsApp वर सुमारे 30
   > मिनिटांचा व्हिडिओ कॉल करेन. तुम्ही फोनची स्क्रीन शेअर करून ॲपमध्ये काही कामं करून पाहाल.
   > आम्ही ॲप तपासतो, तुम्हाला नाही. तुमची परवानगी असेल तर फक्त टीमसाठी शेअर केलेली स्क्रीन आणि
   > आमचे आवाज रेकॉर्ड होतील, 90 दिवस ठेवले जातील. तुम्ही कधीही नकार देऊ शकता किंवा थांबवू शकता."
3. Send only the getting-started tester videos (join, install, sign in, "if something hangs")
   from the Drive pack: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1x51YeyTnpMkbTuKlUQbMYuPIOvldevBi
   Not the ones that show the tasks.
4. Ask them to update Sales X-Ray and WhatsApp from Play, and to charge the phone.

**On the call**
1. **Video-call** from WhatsApp Desktop. Ask them to **share their screen**: in the call, tap
   the share-screen button, then "Start now". If it's missing, update WhatsApp; if the network
   is bad, switch to audio and ask them to say what they see.
2. **Consent** (read it from `07` §3). Only after a clear yes, start recording your laptop
   screen (Windows: Win + Alt + R, or OBS). Never record without the yes.
3. **Warm-up** (3 min): "Today we test the app, not you…" then the calculator practice.
4. **Tasks 1–5** from `07` §4. For each: send the task text, say "start when you're ready",
   start your timer, then stay quiet. Only say "What are you thinking now?" or "hmm". Note
   where they hesitate, wrong taps, Save refused, any Android prompt and their answer.
5. **Task 2 (Call) on video:** a phone call takes over their phone and pauses WhatsApp. Ask
   them to tap Call, check the number shows in the dialler, **end the call at once** and come
   back to the video call. Then go on to Task 3 as if the call happened.
6. **After every task:** the ease question, 1 to 7 (`07a` §1). Then help only if they failed,
   just enough to start the next task.
7. **At the end:** UMUX-Lite (2 questions), the SUS (10, read aloud; they answer 1–5), then
   "What would you change first?". Thank them.

**Within the hour after**
1. Fill one row per task in `07c-score-sheet.csv`, with codes (P01, P02…), never names or
   numbers.
2. Move the recording to AC's restricted research folder, name it by code and date, and delete
   it from the laptop. Put a reminder to delete it after 90 days.
3. Write the top three problems you saw, each with its severity (1–4) and one short quote.
4. Now send them the task videos from the pack, as a thank-you.

**Don'ts:** no managers listening; no real customer's number (the test lead uses AC's test
SIM); no helping during a task; no "good" or "right"; never share a tester's answers with
their employer.
