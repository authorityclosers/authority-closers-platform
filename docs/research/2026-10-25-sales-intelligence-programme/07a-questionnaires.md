# 07a — Questionnaires: SEQ, UMUX-Lite and SUS (English, Hindi, Marathi)

*Part of the usability test kit (chapter 7). **The Hindi and Marathi versions are drafts, to
be checked by a native speaker, and are not validated:** compare their scores only within the
same language over time, never with English norms (chapter 3 §4.5). Read each item aloud, or
send it as a message and let the tester reply with the number.*

**Order in a session:** the SEQ after every task; at the end, UMUX-Lite first (short), then
the SUS. In the in-app survey (chapter 6, idea 13) use only the SEQ and UMUX-Lite.

---

## 1. SEQ, the ease question (after every task)

Scale 1–7. Ask after failed tasks too, and read it together with whether the task passed
(Sauro & Dumas 2009; chapter 3 §4.3).

| | Question | 1 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| EN | Overall, how difficult or easy was this task? | Very difficult | Very easy |
| HI | कुल मिलाकर, यह काम करना कितना मुश्किल या आसान था? | बहुत मुश्किल | बहुत आसान |
| MR | एकूण पाहता, हे काम करणं किती अवघड किंवा सोपं होतं? | खूप अवघड | खूप सोपं |

Card to show or send: `1  2  3  4  5  6  7` with the end words under 1 and 7.

---

## 2. UMUX-Lite (two items, at the end)

Scale 1–7: 1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree (Lewis, Utesch & Maher 2013).

| # | EN | HI | MR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales X-Ray's features meet my needs. | Sales X-Ray की सुविधाएँ मेरी ज़रूरतें पूरी करती हैं। | Sales X-Ray मधल्या सुविधा माझ्या गरजा पूर्ण करतात. |
| 2 | Sales X-Ray is easy to use. | Sales X-Ray इस्तेमाल करने में आसान है। | Sales X-Ray वापरायला सोपं आहे. |

Scale words: EN 1 "strongly disagree", 7 "strongly agree" · HI 1 "बिल्कुल असहमत", 7 "पूरी तरह
सहमत" · MR 1 "पूर्णपणे असहमत", 7 "पूर्णपणे सहमत".

**Score (0–100):** `((item 1 − 1) + (item 2 − 1)) × 100 ÷ 12`.
**On the SUS scale, approximately:** `0.65 × UMUX-Lite + 22.9` (MeasuringU's published
regression, a secondary source; chapter 3 §4.2).

*The original wording of item 1 is "This system's capabilities meet my requirements"; we name
the app and use plain words, as chapter 3 §4.6 allows for everyday equivalence.*

## 3. SUS, the all-positive version (at the end)

The all-positive System Usability Scale behaves like Brooke's standard SUS with fewer
misreadings, which matters in translation (Sauro & Lewis 2011; chapter 3 §4.1). Scale 1–5:
1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree. HI: 1 "बिल्कुल असहमत", 5 "पूरी तरह सहमत". MR: 1
"पूर्णपणे असहमत", 5 "पूर्णपणे सहमत".

| # | English | हिंदी (draft) | मराठी (draft) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I think that I would like to use Sales X-Ray frequently. | मुझे लगता है कि मैं Sales X-Ray अक्सर इस्तेमाल करना चाहूँगा/चाहूँगी। | मला Sales X-Ray वारंवार वापरायला आवडेल असं वाटतं. |
| 2 | I found Sales X-Ray to be simple. | मुझे Sales X-Ray सरल लगा। | मला Sales X-Ray साधं, गुंतागुंत नसलेलं वाटलं. |
| 3 | I thought Sales X-Ray was easy to use. | मुझे Sales X-Ray इस्तेमाल करने में आसान लगा। | मला Sales X-Ray वापरायला सोपं वाटलं. |
| 4 | I think that I could use Sales X-Ray without the support of a technical person. | मुझे लगता है कि मैं किसी तकनीकी व्यक्ति की मदद के बिना Sales X-Ray इस्तेमाल कर सकता/सकती हूँ। | कोणत्याही तांत्रिक व्यक्तीच्या मदतीशिवाय मी Sales X-Ray वापरू शकेन असं मला वाटतं. |
| 5 | I found the various functions in Sales X-Ray were well integrated. | मुझे लगा कि Sales X-Ray के अलग-अलग फ़ीचर आपस में अच्छी तरह जुड़े हुए हैं। | मला Sales X-Ray मधली वेगवेगळी फीचर्स एकमेकांशी चांगली जोडलेली वाटली. |
| 6 | I thought there was a lot of consistency in Sales X-Ray. | मुझे लगा कि पूरे Sales X-Ray में चीज़ें एक जैसे तरीके से काम करती हैं। | मला संपूर्ण Sales X-Ray मध्ये गोष्टी एकसारख्या पद्धतीने चालतात असं वाटलं. |
| 7 | I would imagine that most people would learn to use Sales X-Ray very quickly. | मुझे लगता है कि ज़्यादातर लोग Sales X-Ray बहुत जल्दी सीख लेंगे। | बहुतेक लोक Sales X-Ray खूप लवकर शिकतील असं मला वाटतं. |
| 8 | I found Sales X-Ray very intuitive. | मुझे Sales X-Ray आसानी से समझ में आने वाला लगा। | मला Sales X-Ray सहज समजणारं वाटलं. |
| 9 | I felt very confident using Sales X-Ray. | Sales X-Ray इस्तेमाल करते समय मुझे पूरा भरोसा था। | Sales X-Ray वापरताना मला पूर्ण आत्मविश्वास वाटला. |
| 10 | I could use Sales X-Ray without having to learn anything new. | Sales X-Ray इस्तेमाल करने के लिए मुझे कुछ नया सीखने की ज़रूरत नहीं पड़ी। | Sales X-Ray वापरण्यासाठी मला काहीही नवीन शिकावं लागलं नाही. |

**Score (0–100), all-positive version:** add up `(answer − 1)` for all ten items and multiply
by 2.5. *(The standard SUS alternates positive and negative items and is scored differently;
don't mix the two versions in one round.)*

**Reading a score** (English, standard norms; chapter 3 §4.1): 68 is the average across
products; popular phone apps averaged about 78; around 71 reads as "good" and above about 85
as "excellent". With 5–8 testers a score is a signal, not a benchmark; the quarterly n = 20
study reports it with a confidence interval.

## 5. Before these drafts are used in a benchmark

Follow chapter 3 §4.6 (Beaton et al. 2000): two forward translations, a merge, two blind back
translations, a review with a sales trainer, a pre-test where testers say what each item meant
to them, and a written record. Until then, these are for formative rounds only.

**Sources:** Brooke (1996) SUS; Sauro & Lewis (2011) all-positive SUS; Lewis, Utesch & Maher
(2013) UMUX-Lite; Sauro & Dumas (2009) SEQ. Full references in chapter 3.
