# Independent Review: Sales X-Ray Analysis-Cost Study

**Verdict:** agree with changes

The study correctly identifies the primary cost drivers of today's analysis pipeline: ₹4.97 of fixed LLM overhead per call driven by four redundant JSON outputs, six uncached reads of the transcript, and an inflexible ₹0.367/min transcription floor from ElevenLabs Scribe v2. Consolidating the six downstream extraction stages into a single structured pass is empirically sound and cuts LLM spend by over 70%.

However, the specific "≤ ₹0.40 tomorrow" recommendation (Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite audio transcription + Gemini 3.8 Flash one-pass text analysis on Flex tier) cannot be approved as written. It relies on an untested Flex tier SLA, fails the cost gate on the 4.5-minute production average call length under standard pricing, ignores the announced 2x price increase for Gemini 3.8 Flash taking effect on 1 January 2027, compares the candidate pipeline against an unfair oracle baseline, and suppresses its own benchmark data demonstrating that GPT-6 Luna decisively beats Gemini 3.8 Flash on quality, hallucination rate, and cost.

  - Structure the pipeline with an automatic standard-tier fallback and recalculate the blended cost under expected peak-hour fallback rates.

### Finding 2: Gemini 3.8 Flash Price Doubles on 1 January 2027, Invalidation of the Economic Foundation
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Evidence:**
  - Google Gemini 3.8 Flash pricing doubles from $0.75 / $3.75 to $1.50 / $7.50 per 1M tokens on 1 January 2027 ([`01-cost-anatomy.md` lines 9–10](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/paper/01-cost-anatomy.md#L9-L10), [`02-market.md` line 44](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/paper/02-market.md#L44)), just 72 days after the study date.

  - On 1 January 2027, the analysis fixed cost doubles to ₹4.06 per call and variable to ₹0.0628/min.
  - On a 4.5-minute call, analysis alone on Flex tier will cost `(₹2.03 / 4.5) + ₹0.0314 = ₹0.482/min`. Adding 3.1 Flash-Lite Flex audio transcription (₹0.079/min) yields **₹0.56/min on Flex** (and **₹1.12/min on standard**).
  - The recommended architecture becomes unviable in production within 10 weeks of deployment.
- **Concrete Fix:**
  - Model 2027 list prices in `costmodel.py` and Section 7.
  - Remove Gemini 3.8 Flash as the long-term recommendation for the single-pass extraction stage.
  - Transition the primary analysis recommendation to a model with stable low output pricing (e.g., GPT-6 Luna at $0.10/$0.50 or Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50).

### Finding 3: Methodological Leakage: Comparing Candidate on Noisy ASR Against Baseline on Oracle Transcripts
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Evidence:**
  - In [`06-results.md` line 35](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/paper/06-results.md#L35), the study acknowledges that today's transcriber (ElevenLabs Scribe v2) was not benchmarked ("no ElevenLabs bench key was available").
  - In [`06-results.md` Table 6.3 line 65](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/paper/06-results.md#L65) and [`results/scores/facts.md` lines 27–28](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/results/scores/facts.md#L27-L28), today's 6-pass pipeline was evaluated on `oracle` (perfect human ground truth) transcripts (`text/today/google_gemini-3.8-flash/oracle/run1`).
  - The candidate pipeline was evaluated on noisy transcripts generated by Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite (`text/onepass2/google_gemini-3.8-flash/asr_openrouter_google_gemini-3.1-flash-lite_phone_8k/run1`).
  - Because today's baseline ran on clean text, its measured hallucination rate was 0.3%, while the candidate pipeline measured 5.2%.
  - In [`results/scores/bootstrap.md` line 3](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/results/scores/bootstrap.md#L3), the candidate pipeline shows an unsupported (hallucination) rate increase of **+4.9 percentage points [95% CI: +2.7%, +7.3%]**.
  - Gate M4 requires "Hallucination rate ≤ today" ([`03-benchmark-design.md` line 16](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/paper/03-benchmark-design.md#L16)). The candidate pipeline fails this gate.
  - The author excuses this in [`06-results.md` line 69](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/paper/06-results.md#L69) by asserting "today's pipeline pays the same price through Scribe's errors, which this round could not measure." This is an unproven assertion that violates paired evaluation rigor.
- **Concrete Fix:**
  - Execute today's 6-pass pipeline on the exact same Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite ASR transcripts used for the candidate arms to achieve an apples-to-apples comparison.
  - Alternatively, transcribe the 16 fictional calls with Scribe v2 to evaluate both pipelines on their real intended transcription inputs.
  - Do not claim Gate M4 passes until both arms are scored on identical transcription inputs.

### Finding 12: High-Impact Cost Engineering Tactics Missed by the Study
- **Severity:** minor
- **Evidence:**
  - **Context Caching:** [`01-cost-anatomy.md` line 28](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/paper/01-cost-anatomy.md#L28) notes "Cached tokens: 0 on every receipt." Static instructions (prompts, schemas, profile JSON) total ~6,000 tokens per request. Gemini implicit/explicit prefix caching ($0.075/M vs $0.75/M) or Anthropic prompt caching (90% discount) would save 30–50% of input token costs.
  - **Local Voice Activity Detection (VAD):** Gemini audio-in bills 32 tokens per second regardless of whether the audio contains speech or dead air. Sales calls contain 20–35% silence. Running an open-source, local VAD (e.g. Silero-VAD in <100ms) to strip pauses >1.5s prior to upload would cut audio billing duration and latency by 20–30% with zero quality loss.
  - **Schema and Key Compression:** Output tokens cost 5× input tokens on Gemini ($3.75 vs $0.75) and account for 68% of LLM spend ([`01-cost-anatomy.md` line 85](file:///home/acdev/research/analysis-cost-20261020/review/paper/01-cost-anatomy.md#L85)). Compacting JSON keys or adopting structured YAML/TOML reduces output token volume by 25–40%.
  - **Call Triage Routing:** 40% of inbound/outbound calls are under 4 minutes (e.g., gatekeeper brush-offs, voicemails). Implementing a fast local classifier to route short calls to a minimal single-pass extraction avoids running 27 pointer reviews and heavy schemas where unnecessary.
- **Concrete Fix:**
  - Add a dedicated "Immediate Engineering Optimizations" section covering local VAD silence trimming, static prefix context caching, and schema key minimization.
