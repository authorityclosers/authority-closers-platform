Sales X-Ray call analysis: from ₹1.50 to ₹0.15–0.29 a minute

Today's pipeline transcribes with Scribe and reads the transcript six times with Gemini 3.8 Flash: ₹1.19 a minute on staging receipts, ₹1.50 on seven real calls. One analysis pass on a Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite transcript does the same job far cheaper. On 16 fictional calls with known facts, GPT-6 Luna's one pass finds 20 points more facts than today's six stages reading the same transcript. On seven real calls the coaching lead annotated, Gemini 3.8 Flash's one pass covers his notes as well as today, and with his Brain 4.0 rubric and timestamped coach notes it covers more (+4 points); Luna covers about 5 points fewer, at a fifth of Gemini's price. Recommended: Flash-Lite → Luna, ₹0.29 a minute at standard speed and ₹0.15 on the half-price Flex and batch tiers; for the best coaching, Gemini 3.8 Flash on the same prompt, ₹0.50 (₹0.25).

The ₹0.10 configuration, in the product's full formats

Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite transcribes on Gemini's Flex tier; GPT-6 Luna answers the one-pass build's two requests (facts with call data and the 27-pointer review; the C5 report, with a report-depth instruction) on OpenAI's Flex tier. Measured on the fictional calls: ₹0.108 a minute at 13.8 minutes, ₹0.083 at 60, ₹0.18 at 4.5, ready in about 5–10 minutes, every C5 report and pointer review valid with the report-depth prompt, facts above today's and coaching within noise of it (section 6). With Gemini writing the report instead, coaching is 4 points above today for ₹0.155. Real calls are denser (₹0.12 a minute for transcription on Flex), so the same set-up costs about ₹0.15 there until the transcript output is made compact. Cap Luna's output at about 20k tokens with one retry: one request in 36 ran away.

Why Lean, and what it gives up

It finds the most facts (the CRM, the prospect profile and the call data all hang on them) with the fewest unsupported ones, costs a fifth of today, and does not double in January. Its coaching covers about 5 points fewer of the coaching lead's notes than today's, an interval that still includes zero. The coaching will change anyway: the Brain 4.0 rubric and the coaching lead's annotated calls are the material to close that gap, as an evaluation set first. If the best coaching matters on day one, run "Both": Luna's facts and scores with Gemini's coach notes and report, ₹0.25 a minute more (₹0.12 on Flex).

Tomorrow, on today's pipeline, with nothing new to approve: about ₹0.6 a minute

Flex tier on every background Gemini call (same model, half price, answers in 1–15 minutes), stop the deep pipeline's per-segment LLM labels (27% of today's bill, not used by the report), deduplicate re-analysis and no-op the 5-minute pointer timer on unchanged reports. Scribe stays, so this cannot go below ₹0.40: Scribe alone is ₹0.37.

What the real calls taught

  • One pass loses nothing on the same transcript. Gemini's one pass equals today's six stages on the real calls (−0.3 points) and on the fictional ones (6.7), at a sixth of the analysis cost. With the Brain 4.0 rubric, measured pauses and timestamped coach notes it covers 4 points more than today (50.0% against 46.0%).
  • Measure delivery, don't ask a model to hear it. On the call the coaching lead faulted for having no pauses, the measured silence share is 6.8% against 22% on the call he praised for its pauses. Gemini Flash-Lite, listening to the same audio, said the salesperson "uses pauses effectively", and feeding its impressions to Luna lowered its coverage (33.7% against 37.0% without them). Pauses measured locally are accurate and free; on their own they did not move Luna's coverage (36.7%), so the value is in correct evidence for Tone, Pace & Pauses.
  • A bigger model is not the lever. Claude Sonnet 5.5 (₹1.54 a minute) and GPT-6.1 Sol (₹0.85) on the same Brain 4.0 prompt covered no more of the coaching lead's notes than Gemini 3.8 Flash. Asking for timestamped coach notes (10–20 moments, strengths and changes) lifted Luna by about 4 points, as much as switching to Sonnet at 30 times the price.
  • Judges disagree by up to 20 points and favour their own model family, so every real-call number is a mean of judges from two families; a third (Claude Sonnet on the subscription, no API spend) checks the main rows.
  • The ₹0.10 path is now transcription. Flex halves it to ₹0.12; a compact transcript format, or self-hosted IndicConformer (≈ ₹0.02), would take Lean below ₹0.10.

5 · Results · real calls, scored against the coaching lead

Seven real calls, judged against the coaching lead's own notes

The coaching lead listened to seven real sales calls (9–24 minutes, Hindi, Marathi and English; 108 minutes in all) and wrote 81 timestamped notes on six of them: what the salesperson did well, what to improve, and what the buyer signalled. Those notes are the gold standard here. Every pipeline read the same Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite transcripts. Judges from different model families (Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna on every pipeline; Gemini 3.8 Flash on most; Claude Sonnet through the Claude subscription, at no API cost, on the main ones) marked each note as covered, partly covered or missed by the pipeline's coaching output, and listed claims the transcript contradicts. 9 · Method

How the benchmark was built

  • Calls: 16 fictional calls, 2–48 minutes (264 audio minutes), all eight call types, Indian English, Hinglish, Marathi–English and Hindi; written by three model families (Codex, Gemini, Claude) so no tested family grades only its own writing. 26–60 facts per call with verbatim evidence, needles at 10/50/90% depth, traps, 2–6 planted coaching truths, and a reference report in the product's v4 schema. A checker rejects phone numbers, emails, IDs and real brand names.
  • Audio: Kokoro voices on GitHub's free runners (Marathi with Marathi phonemes); clean and phone versions; true turn timings for speaker scoring. Real-speech check: FLEURS Hindi and Marathi.
  • Today's pipeline was not re-implemented: the harness imports the product's own prompt builders read-only and sends exactly those requests (C4, C5, pointer review, call-data fast, deep extract, deep labels).
  • Spend: $12.5 of the $20 OpenRouter credit; Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Sarvam and Groq on free credit or free tier; GitHub runners free. No real customer or staff audio left the providers already in use.
  • Hybrid (shape d): a small embedding classifier trained on 1,253 labelled segments agreed with the LLM's segment labels only 33–61% of the time (near the majority-class baseline), so per-segment labels can't move to a local model on this data; measured audio features stay local as they are today.

11 · Limits

What this round cannot show

  • Scribe was not measured (no bench key; the production key was not used without approval), so the end-to-end comparison is against today's pipeline on a perfect transcript, which flatters today.
  • Synthetic calls are cleaner than real ones, Marathi voices carry a Hindi accent, and FLEURS is read speech. A real-call check on staging staff calls needs the owner's approval.
  • Flex was not called: its prices are half of the measured standard prices; latency, fallbacks and availability are unmeasured. The 2.5-minute call stays at ₹0.50/min even on Flex.
  • Scoring favours the new format: the one-pass arms write facts in the benchmark's own types, today's facts are mined from its fact stages only, and judges drop roles and anchors. Some rows are judged by the same model family that wrote them.
  • 16 calls, 24 needles: needle and trap results are inconclusive; the speaker metric is a rough time-overlap mapping, not DER.
  • Costs keep the last successful request per call and stage (research reruns excluded, production retries would be billed).
